QUIZ DESIGN


Questions must use MCQ format.


Example:

⭐ What is 8 × 3?


A. 21
B. 24
C. 26
D. 28


Each answer must be a large touch-friendly button.


When the child selects an answer:


Correct:



- show positive visual feedback
- show “Correct! 🎉”
- award stars
- play a short success animation/sound if sound assets are available



Wrong:



- show “Oops! 😄”
- clearly show the correct answer
- do not confuse the child
- then allow the child to continue



Never allow multiple answers to be selected for the same question.


After answering, show a clear Next button or automatically continue only if the interaction remains understandable for children.
